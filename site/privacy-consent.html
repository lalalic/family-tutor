Privacy & consent

A clear starting point for family conversations.

These notes describe the pilot’s intended privacy model. They are product communication, not a substitute for the final terms, consent language, or legal review required before accepting families.

Child spaces are private by default

Each child has a separate tutor context and private Discord destination. Parents do not normally have access to the child’s tutor channel and are not routinely watching individual messages.

Parents receive useful signals

Family Tutor may share concise learning telemetry such as topics studied, evidence of understanding, recurring misconceptions, progress, missed plans, next steps, or when parental support may help. Routine child messages, casual conversation, and full transcripts are not mirrored.

Safety escalation

Serious safety concerns are an exception. If escalation is necessary, Family Tutor shares only the minimum information needed for a parent or responsible adult to respond appropriately. When safe and appropriate, the child is told that escalation is happening.

Data boundaries

The family owns its ChatGPT account and Projects. Hosted Family Tutor services are intended to provide thin routing, connectivity, and support infrastructure. Logs should contain redacted operational metadata rather than child content. Families can ask about deletion or export through hello@qili2.com; the pilot operator will confirm the available process and retention schedule before participation.

Before the pilot begins

We will confirm guardian consent, privacy notice, terms, support hours, retention, incident handling, pricing, and deletion/export procedures with each participating family. These decisions require owner and, where appropriate, counsel approval.
