A better boundary

Private enough for honest questions. Visible enough for care.

Children need room to ask imperfect questions. Parents need confidence that learning is moving in a good direction. Family Tutor keeps those needs distinct.

  • Each child has a separate tutor space by default.
  • Parent updates are concise learning signals—not routine transcripts.
  • Serious safety concerns are the exception, with minimum-necessary escalation.
  • Your family owns its ChatGPT account and Projects.

Questions families ask

Clear answers before you decide.

Is Family Tutor a curriculum subscription?

No. Your family brings its own ChatGPT account and Projects. Family Tutor is the setup, connection, privacy, and support layer around a personalized family tutor.

Can parents read every message?

Parents do not normally have access to a child’s tutor channel and are not routinely watching individual messages. Family Tutor may share concise learning signals; serious safety concerns may require minimum-necessary escalation.

What does setup involve?

The intended path is guided setup and an acceptance check. We agree the family scope and support window before participation; we do not promise zero setup or universal compatibility.

Will this replace me, a teacher, or a tutor?

No. It is a support layer for everyday questions and learning follow-up. Adults and educators remain important for judgment, care, and situations needing human help.

What is included in the pilot?

The pilot is intentionally small. Exact scope, support, privacy terms, and pricing are confirmed with each family before participation.
