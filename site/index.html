How it works

Your tools. Your family. One thoughtful layer.

Family Tutor connects the pieces you already use into a dependable routine for learning, reflection, and support.

Give each child room

Every child gets an independent tutor thread and private channel. Their companion can remember the learning context that matters.

◌

Keep parents in the loop

Parents receive concise telemetry: what was studied, what clicked, what needs practice, and where support may help.

Trust is part of the product

Private enough for honesty. Visible enough for care.

Children need space to ask imperfect questions. Parents need confidence that learning is moving in a good direction. Family Tutor keeps those needs distinct.

  • Child channels are private to the child and tutor by default.
  • Routine parent updates are learning signals, not transcript mirroring.
  • Serious safety concerns are the exception, with minimum-necessary escalation.
  • Your family owns its ChatGPT account and Projects.

Questions families ask

Clear answers before you decide.

Is Family Tutor an education-content subscription?

No. Your family brings its own ChatGPT account and Projects. Family Tutor is the setup, connection, privacy, and support layer around a personalized AI tutor.

Can parents read every message?

Parents do not normally have access to a child’s tutor channel and are not routinely watching individual messages. Family Tutor may share concise learning telemetry with the parent channel, and serious safety concerns may require minimum-necessary escalation.

What does the pilot include?

The pilot includes a guided setup, child-specific Project bindings, shared Discord routing, an acceptance test, and an agreed support window. Exact scope and pricing are confirmed before participation.

Is this ready for every family today?

We are deliberately starting with a small pilot. Some operational, legal, and business details still require review before broad public launch. We’ll be candid about what is and is not ready.
